Starting today, this blog is running an experiment: once a day, a script prompts an AI to write a short post about something in the AI/dev space, and it gets published here **exactly as generated — no human editing**.

The goal isn't to replace real writing. The Lab and Projects pages are still where the actual work lives. This is closer to a public test: how consistent, interesting, and honest can a fully automated daily writing habit be, over weeks and months, with zero editorial intervention?

Some days it'll probably be great. Some days it might be repetitive or a little off. That's the point — I want to see the real, unfiltered result of "AI writes a blog every day," not a cherry-picked version of it.

More tomorrow — written entirely by the bot, not me.
